Globe Wars (prototype)

  • Every node is a falling-sand world that runs on Sandfall's own rules. Volcanoes melt sand into lava that cools into 🔷 glass, gardens grow 🌿 plants in the rain, fungal caves grow 🍄 fungus on steam. Gold reapers eat glass, plants and fungus that touch them; the eating spreads through everything connected to it, like fire through a plant bed, and each cell eaten is one resource.
  • Units are a swarm of glowing bugs that live in the globe. Hatch them next to a core (10 resources each, any mix) with the army bubble. Drag from one of your globes to a linked one on the map to send half its bugs, or use the army bubble: they fly to the gate and through it.
  • Gates: every link is a pair of doors in the glass, each on the side facing the neighbour. Open one of yours (army bubble) and whatever touches it pours through into the linked globe: sand, water, lava and acid fall downhill into globes below you on the map, steam, fire and fumes rise into globes above. Lava, acid and fumes eating at a core wear it down, and when it falls the globe goes to whoever poured them in. You can't close a rival's gate, but you can wall off the inside of yours.
  • Bugs coming through a gate head for the core, chewing through sand and plants fast, glass slowly and stone very slowly. Fire, lava and magma burn them, acid eats them, water drowns them, and bugs of different owners fight (defenders win more often). Each one that reaches the core takes a bite out of it; when the core's ❤ runs out the globe is captured. So build defences: walls, moats, fire.
  • Strikes: from the army bubble of a globe next to one of yours, drop fire rain, acid rain, a virus or a meteor into it. A roof keeps rain out.
  • Every globe starts with a small factory and a few generator pieces in stock: place them (and buy more) to make much more.
  • Tap a globe to open it. Like the sandbox, the elements bubble holds the elements, brushes and brush size, and Move & zoom; two fingers zoom and pan with any tool. Each globe has its own limited stock of every element (the number on it): drawing uses it up, Dig picks cells back up into it, and you can buy more with your resources, generators included, to build any factory in any globe.
  • Upkeep every 10 s grows with your globes and units and is paid from your biggest stock. Can't pay, and units desert.
  • Red and Green expand and fight each other too. Below, choose how hard they go after you. Take all their globes to win. Every globe runs all the time.
