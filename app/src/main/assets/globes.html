Globe Wars (prototype)

  • Every node is a falling-sand world. Volcanoes melt sand into lava that cools into 🔷 glass, salt pans boil brine into 🧂 salt, fungal caves grow mushrooms that puff 🍄 spores. Gold reapers harvest what touches them.
  • Train units in your globes (30 resources each, any mix). Drag from one of your globes to a linked one on the map to send half its units, or use the buttons inside a globe.
  • Units arriving at an enemy or neutral globe fight its garrison. The defenders get a defence bonus from what surrounds their core: stone and glass walls, and lava, magma or acid in a ring around it.
  • Shape your globes: each globe has its own limited matter. Dig picks cells up into that globe's stock, then pick a material and drag to place it. Buy more with your resources: stone walls, acid, reapers, and generators (magma, sand spouts, brine springs, steam vents, mycelium) to build any factory in any globe.
  • Upkeep every 10 s grows with your globes and units and is paid from your biggest stock. Can't pay, and units desert.
  • Red and Green expand and fight each other too. Below, choose how hard they go after you. Take all their globes to win. Only the globe you're looking at and its neighbours run live; the rest produce at their measured rate.
