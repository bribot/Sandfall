Globe Wars (prototype)

  • Every node is a tiny falling-sand world. Tap one to look inside.
  • Volcano globes melt sand into lava that cools into 🔷 glass. Salt pans boil brine into 🧂 salt. Fungal caves grow mushrooms that puff 🍄 spores.
  • Reapers (gold blocks) harvest the product that touches them. Build more in your globes to earn more.
  • To capture a globe next to one of yours, open it and rain acid on it, paid with salt and spores in any mix. When half of its glowing core is eaten, it's yours. A rain usually takes a neutral globe; owned ones need a storm or two.
  • Defend with shields: drag in your own globe to draw them. Acid only slowly etches through.
  • Upkeep: every 10 s your empire costs resources, taken from whatever you have most of. It grows faster than your territory. Can't pay, and your weakest core crumbles; a crumbled globe can break away.
  • Red and Green expand too, and fight each other. Take every globe they own to win.
  • Only the globe you're viewing, its neighbours and globes under attack run live; the rest pause and produce at their measured rate.
